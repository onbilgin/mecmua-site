Mecmua for publishers

Mecmua is a personal reader. It typesets the feeds a reader subscribes to into a private, magazine-style issue on their own iPad — and it is built to respect your rules and your work.

In short: issues are for the reader's personal use only and cannot be exported or shared. Every article is credited to its author and source and links back to the original. Mecmua follows robots.txt, never bypasses paywalls, and you can opt out at any time.

How your content appears

  • Only for the person who subscribed. A reader adds your feed (or picks it from the catalog); Mecmua then prepares issues for that reader alone, on their device. There is no Mecmua server, cache or public archive of your articles.
  • No redistribution. Issues cannot be exported, shared or sent out of the app. The only things a reader can share are an article's link and their own notes.
  • Credit and links. Each article shows its title, author and source, and opens your original page with one tap. Each issue's colophon states that all articles belong to their authors and publishers.
  • No ads, no tracking. Mecmua shows no advertising around your content and collects no data about readers.

How Mecmua fetches

  • Feeds: Mecmua reads the RSS/Atom feeds you publish for subscription, like any feed reader.
  • Full text: when a feed carries only a summary, Mecmua may fetch the article page so the reader can read it in full. Before doing so it checks your robots.txt (RFC 9309). If the page is disallowed, Mecmua uses only what your feed provides.
  • Paywalls and access controls: responses such as 401, 402, 403 and 451, and pages marked as paywalled, are treated as final. Mecmua never logs in, never uses another reader's access and never works around a paywall.
  • Pace: requests to the same host are spaced at least one second apart, and a Crawl-delay you set is honored (up to 10 seconds).
  • Identification: requests come directly from the reader's iPad and identify themselves with this user agent: 
    Mecmua/1.0 (iPadOS; personal feed reader)

Opting out of full-text fetching

Add a group for the Mecmua product token to your robots.txt. Mecmua will then show only the text in your feed. For example, to block all article pages:

User-agent: Mecmua
Disallow: /

Or only a section of your site:

User-agent: Mecmua
Disallow: /premium/

If you have no group for Mecmua, the rules for User-agent: * apply. Changes take effect the next time a reader's app refreshes your site's rules.

The built-in catalog

To help readers get started, Mecmua includes a catalog of suggested sources that publish open feeds. Being listed does not mean any endorsement or partnership, and no catalog entry is paid.

  • To have your site removed from the catalog, or its name, description or feed address corrected, write to us. We apply requests in the next app update.
  • Removing a site from the catalog does not stop individual readers from adding your public feed themselves, as with any feed reader. To limit full-text fetching, use robots.txt as above.

Contact

For any request or concern — catalog changes, attribution, or how your content is displayed — write to mecmuaapp@icloud.com. Please include your site's address. We usually reply within 2 business days.
