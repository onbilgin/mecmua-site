Frequently asked questions

How do I add a source?

In Sources, tap Add Source and enter a website address or an RSS/Atom feed URL. Mecmua finds the site's feed for you. You can also pick ready-made sources from the catalog or paste a Substack profile link.

Can I bring my subscriptions from another RSS reader?

Yes. Export an OPML file from your old reader and import it in Sources. You can export your Mecmua sources as OPML the same way.

How is an issue (magazine or book) made?

In Publications, tap New Publication and choose its sources, template (book, magazine, academic) and schedule. When you tap Generate Issue Now, or when it is due, a new issue appears in your library.

Why didn't my scheduled issue arrive on time?

iPadOS schedules background work based on battery and network conditions. Make sure Mecmua is enabled in Settings → General → Background App Refresh and Low Power Mode is off. Late issues are prepared as soon as you open the app.

Why do some articles show only a summary?

Mecmua uses the feed's text first and fetches the full article page when needed. If the site disallows this in robots.txt, or the article is behind a paywall, Mecmua respects that and shows only the text in the feed.

An article didn't fit into the issue. Where is it?

When the size limit is reached, articles that didn't fit are listed at the back of the issue, each linked to its original source. They also appear under Left Out of This Issue in the issue details. Free issues run up to 40 pages; with Mecmua Pro an issue grows until every article fits.

What does Mecmua Pro add?

The free version includes 10 sources and 1 publication that comes out automatically every week, with issues of up to 40 pages. Pro removes the source and publication limits and adds daily and monthly schedules, the academic template, design options, larger issues and notes export.

My Pro purchase doesn't show up on my new iPad.

Go to Settings → Mecmua Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPad, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Mecmua and tap Cancel Subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the current period.

Can I get a refund?

Apple processes all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my notes and highlights stored? How do I back them up?

Everything stays on your iPad; we have no servers. Your iPad's iCloud or computer backup includes this data. You can export your notes from Notes (Pro).

Do you collect personal data?

No. See the Privacy Policy for details.
